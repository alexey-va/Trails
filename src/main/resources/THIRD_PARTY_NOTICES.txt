- Kotlin standard library 2.4.10 — Apache License 2.0
  Source: https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin
  License text: META-INF/licenses/Apache-2.0.txt
- JetBrains Java annotations 13.0 — Apache License 2.0
  Source: https://github.com/JetBrains/java-annotations
  License text: META-INF/licenses/Apache-2.0.txt
- arc-core and arc-core-paper 2.0.2 — Apache License 2.0
  Source: https://github.com/alexey-va/arc-core
  License text: META-INF/licenses/Apache-2.0.txt
- bStats base and Bukkit 3.2.1 — MIT License
  Source: https://github.com/Bastian/bStats
  License text: META-INF/licenses/bStats-MIT.txt

packages are relocated to private Trails namespaces. Trails itself is distributed under
the Unlicense; its complete text is in META-INF/licenses/Trails-UNLICENSE.txt.
